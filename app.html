Settings

Settings are saved on this iPad only.

Where do I find it?
  1. Open calendar.google.com in a web browser (not the iPad Calendar app)
  2. Top right ⚙ → Settings
  3. Under “Settings for my calendars”, pick your roster calendar
  4. Scroll to Integrate calendar → Secret address in iCal format → copy
  5. Paste it above, tap “Test”, then “Save”

This address can only read your calendar, but anyone who has it can read it — keep it private.
